NYRO Tasks v1.0.21

- Swipe the Today graph out of view on any day, even with a short or empty list. Schedule, Revisits, and Quizzes stay pinned at the top.
- The plus button opens the right form for your current section. Log a mistake from Mistakes, add a lecture from Timetable, or add a weekly quiz or mock from the matching quiz section.
- Open a lecture or quiz to see details. Use the three-dot menu to edit or delete it from Today, Timetable, or Quizzes.
- Lecture date edits shift linked unfinished revisits. Deleting a lecture also removes its unfinished revisits and keeps completed recall history. Deleting a quiz keeps logged traps.
